Rust Discord Bot

Rust+ Login

1. Link your Steam / Rust+ account

Preparing your secure session…

A popup will open to companion-rust.facepunch.com. Sign in with Steam. When it finishes, your account is linked automatically.

2. Pair in game

After linking, open Rust+ in game and pair your server and smart devices. The bot receives the pairing notifications and connects within a few seconds.

Manual fallback

If the popup login can't capture your token (browser security), generate credentials with the rustplus.js CLI (npx @liamcottle/rustplus.js fcm-register) and paste the contents of rustplus.config.json below.
